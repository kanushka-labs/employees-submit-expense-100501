# Approvals

## Purpose

Managers review their team's submitted expense claims and approve or reject
each one.

Needs: F1.

## User Stories

- F2.1 As a manager, I see my team's pending claims in one list, oldest first.
- F2.2 As a manager, I open a claim to view its date, amount, category and receipt.
- F2.3 As a manager, I approve a claim.
- F2.4 As a manager, I reject a claim with a required reason.
- F2.5 As an employee, I receive an email when my claim is approved or rejected.

## Decisions

- A claim is approved or rejected by the employee's line manager.
- Rejecting a claim requires a reason; approving does not.
- A manager acts on claims one at a time; there is no bulk approve/reject.
- The employee is notified by email on each decision.

## Out of Scope

- Bulk approve/reject actions.
- Delegating approval to a deputy when the manager is unavailable.
